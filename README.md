📘 Project Overview

The **2G Mobile Network Jammer Project** was developed as an academic electronics project to study oscillator circuits, frequency generation, transistor-based amplification, and antenna-based RF transmission concepts.

The circuit uses an **NE555 Timer IC** to generate an oscillating signal. This signal is processed through a transistor-based circuit and connected to an antenna section.

The project demonstrates the basic concepts involved in:

⚡ Oscillator circuits  
📡 RF signal generation concepts  
🔧 Transistor-based electronic circuits  
📶 Antenna-based signal transmission  
🧪 Controlled electronics experimentation  

---

 🎯 Aim

To design and study a **2G mobile network jammer circuit concept** using an **NE555 timer IC, transistor-based oscillator circuitry, and an antenna section** for controlled academic experimentation.

 🔌 Circuit Diagram Explanation 

The circuit consists of two main sections:

 🔹 NE555 Timer Section

The NE555 Timer IC is configured to generate an oscillating output signal.

The timing components include resistors and capacitors that control the operation of the timer.

 🔹 Oscillator and RF Section

The output signal is connected to a transistor-based oscillator section.

The oscillator circuit includes:

• Transistor  
• Inductors  
• Capacitors  
• Variable capacitor  
• Antenna connection  

The LC network is used to study resonant frequency generation and RF oscillator principles.

---

 ⚙️ Working Principle

 🔹 Power Supply

The circuit is powered using a DC supply.

When the switch is activated, power is supplied to the NE555 Timer IC and oscillator circuit.

---

 🔹 NE555 Timer Operation

The NE555 Timer IC operates as an oscillator and generates a periodic electrical signal.

The timing components connected to the timer influence the generated signal.

---

 🔹 Transistor Oscillator Stage

The generated signal is coupled to a transistor-based electronic stage.

The transistor, inductors, capacitors, and tuning components form an oscillator network used to study high-frequency electronic signal generation.

---

 🔹 LC Resonant Circuit

The inductors and capacitors create a resonant circuit.

The resonant frequency depends on the values of the inductors and capacitors used in the circuit.

This section demonstrates the fundamental principles of:

🔄 Oscillation  
📡 Resonance  
⚡ Frequency generation  

---

🔹 Antenna Section

The circuit includes an antenna connection for studying antenna-based RF signal transmission in an authorized and controlled environment.
